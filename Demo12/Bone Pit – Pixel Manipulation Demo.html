Move A/D or ←/→ · Jump W/↑/Space (press twice to double jump) · Punch J, K or Z · Pause P

Recolor on level-upEach level rebuilds the player sheet: getImageData, loop the RGBA array, set a new hue in HSL, putImageData.
Enemy tiersEnemy sheets are hue-rotated per wave, so tougher waves look different without new art.
Hit flashA cached all-white silhouette of every sheet is swapped in for a few frames when a body is hit.
Pixel dissolveOn a KO, each opaque pixel of the current frame becomes a particle that flies apart.
