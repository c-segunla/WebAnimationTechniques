Module 9 Key Concepts Reference

1. Coordinate System Transformation

Transformations do not move the drawn object directly; they transform the underlying canvas coordinate grid! Subsequent draw commands render relative to the new grid state.

2. Save & Restore Stack

ctx.save() pushes current matrix/styles onto a stack. ctx.restore() pops and resets state, preventing transform leakage.
